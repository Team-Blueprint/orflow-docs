After a customer completes their first successful payment, Orflow provisions a self-service billing portal linked to their subscription. This portal lets customers manage their account without contacting your support team.

## Access Credentials

On first successful payment, Orflow sends an automated transactional email to the customer. This email includes:

- A unique portal access URL
- A 6-digit temporary entry PIN

The link follows this pattern:

```
{{ SITE_URL }}/portal/access/{portal_token_slug}
```

### Email Delivery

The email is sent asynchronously via Brevo (Sendinblue) with the following characteristics:

- **Format** — Light-mode HTML with inline styles, no images or tracking pixels
- **From** — Orflow's default sender address
- **Subject** — "Your portal access credentials"
- **Redelivery** — Subsequent purchases by the same customer reuse existing credentials. No duplicate email is sent.

## Portal Workflow

The self-service portal is a client-side application hosted on the same domain as the checkout pages.

### Authentication

The portal authenticates customers using a two-step flow:

1. **Access link** — The customer clicks the portal URL, which includes their `portal_token_slug`
2. **PIN entry** — The customer enters their 6-digit temporary PIN
3. **Session token** — On success, the system issues a short-lived JWT session token (configurable expiry) scoped to that customer and their active subscription

### Dashboard

After authentication, the customer sees their billing dashboard with the following sections:

#### Plan Summary

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Plan name** | The name of the subscribed plan |
| **Price** | Amount and billing interval |
| **Status badge** | Current subscription state (active, paused, past_due, etc.) |
| **Next charge date** | Date of the upcoming scheduled payment |

#### Payment Method

Displays the last four digits and card brand of the saved payment instrument. The customer can:

- **View** — See the masked card number and brand (e.g., "•••• •••• •••• 4242 Visa")
- **Update** — Replace with a new card using a secure inline form

#### Payment History

A transaction table showing recent billing records:

- Date of each charge
- Amount and currency
- Payment status (paid / failed)
- Receipt reference

When billing records are empty (e.g., during a trial period), the table shows an appropriate empty state message.

### Subscription Actions

Customers can modify their subscription from the portal based on the current state:

| Action | Available When | Effect |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| **Pause** | `active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, `dunning` | Billing stops. Subscription enters `paused` state. Can be resumed later. |
| **Resume** | `paused` | Billing restarts. Subscription returns to `active`. |
| **Cancel** | `active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, `dunning` | Subscription enters `canceled` state. Access continues until end of current billing period. Irreversible. |
| **Update card** | Any state with an active subscription | Replaces the saved payment method. Useful when payments fail or the card expires. |

### Payment Method Updates

When a customer updates their card:

1. The portal calls `POST /v1/portal/subscriptions/create-update-card-checkout`
2. Orflow creates a $0 Nomba hosted checkout with `tokenizeCard=true` and returns a `checkout_link`
3. The customer is redirected to Nomba's hosted checkout page to tokenize their new card
4. After tokenization, Nomba redirects back to the portal callback URL
5. The portal calls `POST /v1/portal/subscriptions/confirm-update-card` with the `order_reference`
6. Orflow extracts the tokenized card data, creates a new payment method, and sets it as default
7. The customer's display metadata (last four digits, card brand) is updated immediately

### Status Alerts

The portal shows contextual banners for non-healthy states:

- **Past due / dunning** — "Your last payment failed" with an inline button to update the payment method
- **Suspended** — "Your subscription is suspended" with a **Reactivate subscription** button that opens the card update flow

## Best Practices

- **Communicate the portal** — Reference the self-service portal in your post-purchase confirmation pages or receipts so customers know they can self-serve.
- **Monitor subscription events** — Webhook events (`subscription.activated`, `subscription.paused`, `subscription.canceled`) fire when customers take action in the portal. Use them to keep your internal systems in sync.
- **PIN security** — The portal PIN is a time-limited credential. Customers can change it after first login via the **Update PIN** option.
- **Test the flow** — Use `sk_test` keys to purchase a test subscription and walk through the portal end-to-end before going live.

<Note>
The portal session token expires after a configurable period (default: 60 minutes). The customer must re-authenticate with their access link and PIN after expiry.
</Note>
