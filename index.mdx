title: "Introduction to Orflow"
description: "High-performance subscription billing engine built for modern developer-first SaaS products."
---

Orflow is an open-source, developer-centric subscription billing engine that simplifies recurring payments, subscriber onboarding, and plans management. Powered by **Nomba**, Orflow handles complex billing architectures—from trial cycles and plan upgrades to card tokenization and multi-currency localized checkout—allowing you to focus entirely on building your core product.

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flexible Plans" icon="calendar">
    Create customizable subscription plans with support for monthly, yearly, and custom recurring cycles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Seamless Checkout" icon="credit-card">
    Generate hosted checkout pages and localized checkout links that support multi-currency payment collection.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Card Tokenization" icon="shield-check">
    Tokenize cards securely to run automated, recurring charges on scheduled intervals without high PCI-compliance overhead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Instant Webhooks" icon="webhook">
    Keep your systems in perfect sync with instant webhook notifications for subscription creations, cancellations, and renewals.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
